Restoring the Environment. Reclaiming Lives.

Rekobo is built on a dual mission: protecting our city from environmental degradation and protecting our children from the dangers of the dumpsites.

Stopping Floods Before They Start.

Unmanaged plastic waste is the primary culprit behind blocked city drainages, turning ordinary rainstorms into destructive floods. When plastics clog waterways, they stagnate and breed waterborne diseases that threaten entire neighborhoods.

By intercepting recyclables directly at the source (homes and businesses), Rekobo ensures waste flows safely to our processing plants instead of littering our streets.

From Scavenging to Schooling.

Public dumpsters hide a harsh reality: young children and vulnerable youths spending their days digging through hazardous waste to survive. This exposes them to severe health risks and traps them outside the formal education system.

Rekobo actively disrupts this cycle. We provide safe, sanitized employment at our facilities and sponsor educational pathways, turning waste pickers into wage earners and students. Every kilogram you recycle helps fund a brighter, safer future for the next generation.
